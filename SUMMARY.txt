BUSINESS RULES ENFORCED
=============================================================================

1. Bot Capacity Caps (per exchange, paper+live combined)
   - Luno: Maximum 5 bots
   - All others: Maximum 10 bots

2. Profit-Gated Auto-Growth
   - Auto-spawn requires >= R1000 realized profit on that exchange
   - Profit gating is PER EXCHANGE (not global)
   - No more "fill to 65 bots" behavior

3. Reinvestment When At Cap
   - When exchange is at max bots, reinvest instead of spawn

   - Never exceed available funds

5. Unique Bot IDs
   - Every bot has non-null UUID before insert
   - No more duplicate key errors
